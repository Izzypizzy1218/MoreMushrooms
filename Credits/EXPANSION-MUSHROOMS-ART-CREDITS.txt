More Mushrooms — expansion artwork integration v0.5.0
Integrated: 2026-10-10

31 species / 155 runtime PNGs:
- 93 harvested mushroom box images: 01Low, 02Medium, 03Full
- 62 growing mushroom units: two mature forms, A and B, per species

The runtime PNGs are unchanged copies of approved final textures in:
Art/ExpansionMushrooms/v0.1-2026-10-09/Textures/
The display names use the unchanged species identities from:
Art/ExpansionMushrooms/v0.1.1-2026-10-09/species.json

The expansion artwork was generated with the built-in image_gen tool in the
earlier approved art workflow. Referenced mushroom photographs informed
anatomy; they were not pasted into game textures. The source photographs,
authors, licenses, URLs and hashes are retained in the art pack's
PHOTO-CREDITS.txt and references/sources.json. The runtime reference-credit
copy is EXPANSION-MUSHROOMS-PHOTO-CREDITS.txt.

The mushroom box shape follows the existing project template. Its original
credit and MIT license are retained in RIMSHARE-LICENSE.txt.

A and B are different mature mushroom forms, not separate chronological
growth stages. The game's plant renderer changes visual size and the number
of repeated units with growth. No art regeneration or pixel alteration was
performed for the v0.5.0 integration.

Medicinal fungi are collection/trade resources in this release. The artwork
does not itself establish real-world edibility, medicinal efficacy, toxicity,
natural emission of in-game light or ecological simulation rules.
