[English]
v0.6.4
- Prepared cumulative feature information, Korean/English Workshop descriptions and release documentation.
- Gameplay, values and artwork remain unchanged from v0.6.3.
- Passed 2,984 static checks and 130/130 controlled native pawn health/mental-state cases. Preservation comparisons passed with zero unexpected changes.
- Actual harvest, cooking, ingestion, policy and stack checks passed 42/42 in Core and 42/42 with the user's 79 active entries plus the harness. No requested mod IDs were missing.
- Ten actual game frames confirmed all 46 varieties. Some early growth remains low contrast when zoomed out; approved artwork was retained.
- User-mod translation errors and Google HTTP 429 were recorded separately as environment issues. Personal API configuration and existing saves were not copied.
- Ecology tests passed 34 initial censuses and twelve controlled 120-day series with no captured native errors.
- Completed a native-AI day for one pawn each with potatoes, cubensis and panther cap (60,000 native ticks each, no captured errors). Stone-block output of 400/260/0 is one observation per treatment in a small fixture, not a general labor-loss estimate. Existing-save restoration was not rerun; historical verification remains separate.

v0.6.3
- Reworked the Korean and English descriptions of all 21 flavor memories and the three shared hallucination stages.
- Replaced repetitive openings and endings with individual impressions suited to each mushroom's aroma and texture.
- Retained effect labels, mood, durations, cultivation, yields, value, poisoning, treatment, ecology and artwork.

v0.6.2
- Updated nine additional edible flavor descriptions and three shared hallucination descriptions using firsthand food accounts and species-specific sources.
- Retained values and labels, and recorded sources and identification limitations.

v0.6.1
- All five premium wild edibles (matsutake, black truffle, porcini, morel and black trumpet): +15 mood for 48 hours.
- Assorted cultivation now includes cauliflower and purple blewit, for 11 equally likely varieties.
- Standard loss-of-control ranges: liberty cap/cubensis 6–8 hours, fly agaric 8–12 hours, panther cap 12–24 hours.
- New exposures schedule at least 6 hours; an episode including repeated intake is capped at 24 hours from first exposure. Sleep or incapacitation can end wandering early.

v0.6.0
- Increased the share of additional wild varieties while retaining each biome's total base mushroom weight.
- Added species-specific seasons, rare full/partial fairy rings and limited one-time mycelium regrowth.
- Added actual surrounding light for ghost fungus and green-glowing mycena.
- Current content totals 46 varieties with 11 cultivable varieties. Medicinal effects, gathering quests and dedicated growing facilities remain deferred.
