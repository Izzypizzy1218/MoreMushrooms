More Mushrooms adds **46 mushroom varieties** to RimWorld 1.6. This release publishes the accumulated v0.6.0–v0.6.3 changes and the v0.6.4 qualification records. Harmony is required; DLC is optional. Korean and English are included.

- **11 cultivable varieties**, including cauliflower and purple blewit, with equally likely assorted planting and hydroponics support.
- Five premium wild mushrooms give **+15 mood for 48 hours**. The other 16 flavor memories last 6 hours.
- Four psychoactive ingredients can enter normal meals, with recorded per-meal doses, food-policy filtering and species-specific scheduled loss of control between **6 and 24 hours**.
- Wild variety weighting, seasonal selection, rare fairy rings, limited one-time mycelium regrowth and two subtly glowing species.
- Varied Korean/English flavor and hallucination descriptions. v0.6.4 retains v0.6.3 gameplay values, production code, identifiers and approved artwork.

Qualification includes native map generation, controlled 120-day ecological series, 130 clinical cases, actual pawn work, 42 native workflow checks in Core and another 42 with the user's 79 active mods, plus ten rendered frames covering all 46 species. Read the [QA report](https://github.com/Izzypizzy1218/MoreMushrooms/blob/v0.6.4/Docs/QA-v0.6.4.md) for results and scope. These are bounded experiments, not a guarantee for every colony or mod setting. Small, dark growth sprites remain less readable at a distant camera zoom. Startup translation errors and a translation-service HTTP 429 response are reported separately from the workflow results.

For installation, use **MoreMushrooms-v0.6.4.zip**. The `-source.zip` contains development files and provenance; `manifest.json` contains SHA-256 checksums. Remove an older manual copy before extracting the single `MoreMushrooms` folder into `RimWorld/Mods`.

**Steam Workshop was not updated.** Korean and English Workshop text and cumulative change notes are included for the owner to publish manually. Medicinal effects, identification, gathering quests and dedicated substrate/host systems remain deferred.
